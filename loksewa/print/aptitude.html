Administration Aptitude — syllabus and checklist

पहिलो पेपरले पाँच परिवारमा गति र शुद्धता नाप्छ: भूगोल, इतिहास-राजनीति र अर्थतन्त्र, विज्ञान-प्रविधि, मानसिक क्षमता, र भाषा। सही उत्तरमा एक अङ्क, गलतमा त्यसको पाँचौं भाग घट्छ — त्यसैले अनुमान तालिम लिनुपर्ने सीप हो, बानी होइन।

The first paper tests speed with accuracy across five families: geography, history and polity with economy, science and technology, mental ability, and language. One mark for a right answer, and a fifth of it gone for a wrong one — so guessing is a skill you must train, not a habit you must keep.

  • 01 नेपाल र विश्वको भूगोलGeography of Nepal and the world

    • भौतिक संरचनाPhysical set-up
      1. Himal, Pahad, Terai and the internal rivers
      2. River systems: Koshi, Gandaki, Karnali, Mahanadi
      3. Climate zones and their effect on settlement
    • जनसंख्या र बस्तीPopulation and settlement
      1. Census reading, density and migration
      2. Urban and rural settlement patterns
      3. Road, air and river connectivity
    • स्रोत र स्थानको अर्थतन्त्रResources and economy of place
      1. Water, forest, mineral and hydropower potential
      2. Agriculture zones and food security
      3. Disaster geography: flood, landslide, earthquake
    • विश्व भूगोलको आधारWorld geography basics
      1. Continents, oceans and major straits
      2. Climate and vegetation belts
      3. Nepal's position in South Asia and its neighbours

  • 02 इतिहास, राजनीति र अर्थतन्त्रHistory, polity and economy

    • नेपालको आधुनिक इतिहासNepal's modern history
      1. Unification and its administration
      2. Rana period and the 2006 change
      3. Peace process and the constitution
    • राजनीतिको आधार संविधानConstitution as polity
      1. Structure of the 2072 constitution
      2. Fundamental rights and duties
      3. Distribution of power: federation, province, local
    • एक पृष्ठको अर्थतन्त्रEconomy in one page
      1. Three sectors and their share of work
      2. Remittance, trade balance and foreign currency
      3. Budget language: revenue, capital, grant
    • राज्यका निकायState institutions
      1. Executive, legislature, judiciary
      2. Constitutional bodies and their mandate
      3. Independent offices and accountability

  • 03 विज्ञान र प्रविधिScience and technology

    • दैनिक विज्ञानEveryday science
      1. Health, agriculture and environment basics
      2. Disaster science for Nepal
      3. Energy: hydro, solar, and the grid
    • डिजिटल सुशासनDigital governance
      1. E-governance services and their legal base
      2. Data protection and the privacy law
      3. Cyber security awareness for officers
    • समाचारको प्रविधिTechnology in the news
      1. Artificial intelligence in public work
      2. Space, satellites and weather services
      3. What a new technology changes for a citizen

  • 04 मानसिक क्षमता, IQ र तर्कMental ability, IQ and reasoning

    • शाब्दिक तर्कVerbal reasoning
      1. Analogy, classification, series
      2. Coding-decoding
      3. Statement, assumption and conclusion
    • गाणितिक क्षमताQuantitative aptitude
      1. Percentage, profit-loss, interest
      2. Time-work, time-distance
      3. Averages, ratio and proportion
    • तथ्याङ्क व्याख्याData interpretation
      1. Table, bar and pie reading
      2. Sets and Venn diagrams
      3. Speed: what to calculate and what to estimate
    • पजल र बसाइPuzzles and seating
      1. Sequence and arrangement
      2. Blood relations
      3. Clock, calendar and direction

  • 05 भाषा: अङ्ग्रेजी र नेपालीLanguage: English and Nepali

    • कार्यालयको अङ्ग्रेजीEnglish for official work
      1. Grammar in use: tense, agreement, preposition
      2. Vocabulary that appears in papers
      3. Precis, letter and report writing
    • नेपाली भाषा र व्यावहारिक लेखनNepali language and practical writing
      1. शुद्ध लेखन, विराम चिह्न
      2. कार्यपत्र, निवेदन, टिप्पणी शैली
      3. सारांश र भावार्थ
    • दुई भाषाको अनुवादTranslation between the two
      1. Official terms and their pairs
      2. Number, date and name conventions
      3. Reading a law in one language, writing in the other
