01 · THE ACTTOLOG WORLD

One world. Five divisions. A single connected system.

Acttolog connects research, entertainment, learning, play and discovery so an idea can begin as a question, become a lesson, ship as media or a game, and stay supplied with verified tools.

PURPOSE

Acttolog is a digital ecosystem that connects technology, research, education, entertainment, creativity, useful resources, and emerging digital opportunities in one connected world.

VISION

To become a global digital ecosystem where people can easily discover knowledge, technology, entertainment, useful resources, and new opportunities.

MISSION

To make knowledge, technology, research, creativity, entertainment, education, and useful digital resources more accessible by bringing them together through meaningful, practical, and responsible digital experiences.
