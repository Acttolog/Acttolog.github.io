    Studio guide

    A cube that solves itself.

    Every solve is computed live in the browser — nothing is pre-recorded. Switch between the 2×2 pocket cube and the 3×3 classic in the top bar. The 3×3 solves layer by layer; the 2×2 runs a two-phase corner search. The Cube Lab lets you paint any position, checks that a real cube could reach it, and plans the solve.

    3×3 — layer by layer

    1. CrossFour white edges, placed optimally.
    2. CornersWhite corners into the first layer.
    3. F2LMiddle edges finish two layers.
    4. OLLNine yellow stickers face up.
    5. PLLLast-layer pieces permute home.

    2×2 — two phases

    1. OrientAll eight corners twisted upright.
    2. PermuteHalf-turns send every corner home.

    The 2×2 search is exact: a full orientation table (2,187 states) plus a full permutation table (40,320 states) built in your browser in well under a second. Typical solves run about eight moves.

    Cube Lab builder

    Paint one sticker, drag a run, fill a whole face, or switch to Turn and use real layer moves. Type a sequence such as R U R′ U′, pick a preset, or load the cube from the stage. Every build gets a rule check, a verified solve plan and a live 3D preview. Name positions in Library and export a JSON backup.

    Cube Lab

    Paint any cube.

    Paint stickers, drag a run, fill a whole face, or turn real layers with a sequence. Every build is checked as you go, legal positions get a verified solve plan, and the live preview shows the cube in 3D. Built for beginners — deep enough for speedcubers.
